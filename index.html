How to play

  • WASD move, Shift run. Scroll zooms. Q and E turn the view.
  • 1 yellow triangles, 2 red squares, 3 blue spheres, F all of them.
  • Left click throws the chosen creatures toward the cursor. Hold to throw a line of them.
  • Right click whistles at the cursor. Idle and fighting creatures come. Carriers stay at work.
  • Triangles are quick. Squares haul weight and break stone. Spheres swim and wake shrines.
  • Deliver treasures to your heartstone. More points than Vesper when the bell rings.
